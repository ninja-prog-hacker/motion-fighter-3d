MOTION FIGHTER

A camera-controlled 3D training battle. Move in front of your phone and your fighter responds to your gestures.

  • Quick left / right arm extension → punch
  • Both hands raised → block
  • Lower your body → dodge
  • Lift a knee → kick
  • Lean or step sideways → move left / right
Camera permission is needed. Use a secure HTTPS page in your browser.

Safety: keep the phone stable, stand far enough back to stay in frame, and use gentle air movements only. This demo processes pose data in your browser.
